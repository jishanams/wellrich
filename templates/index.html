// About Us

Wellrich – Bringing
Nature to Your Table.

Our intention is not only to reduce the risk caused by adulterated foods, but to build a healthier, conscious community.

Wellrich Agrotech LLP is an urban farming–focused company, registered on December 25, 2019, in Bangalore. The company specializes in producing flowering plants, vegetable saplings, fruit saplings, and a wide range of garden saplings, all cultivated in innovative self-watering pots. Wellrich Agrotech has been actively supplying premium-quality plants to villas, gated communities, apartments, flats, and individual homes across Bangalore, helping customers create sustainable home gardens with ease. Many of our customers have shifted away from toxin-filled vegetables and started growing their own purely organic vegetables, kitchen herbs, and fruits with the help of our easy, self-watering system

Learn Our Story
OUR MISSION

By choosing natural products, you support your health, promote clean and wholesome food, and strengthen the livelihoods of your own people.

For many, what started as a small interest has now grown into a beautiful hobby of gardening and urban farming. Our simple, low-maintenance system (watering only once in 15 days) makes it possible for anyone to grow healthy plants, even with a busy schedule.

We use our own in-house bio-fertilizer and bio-products, ensuring that every plant grown through our system is 100% natural, safe, and organic. By developing and using these biological inputs, we maintain soil health, boost plant growth, and eliminate the need for harmful chemical fertilizers.

This strengthens our mission of creating a healthier society by:

Providing clean, organic produce
Protecting the environment
Encouraging sustainable urban farming
Supporting a toxin-free lifestyle

Our bio-system, combined with our self-watering pots, makes gardening effortless and completely eco-friendly—perfect for villas, apartments, and modern urban homes.
